## Prospective update details

Model calls were logged with token counts; processing took about 7.3 hours (extraction 6.7 hours and verification 36 minutes, including rate-limit retries) at a list-price model cost of $9.15. 182 of the 632 articles contributed more than one case. The lower topic linkage persisted after excluding cardiac MRI (441/792, 55.7%, vs 2,243/3,613, 62.1%) and after adjustment for modality mix (49.9% vs 54.6%). Intervals for the prospective-update comparison come from an article-level cluster bootstrap (2,000 replicates; articles resampled within each group) and are shown with Newcombe intervals that treat figures as independent; a difference is described only when both intervals exclude zero. Within the primary collection, researcher-rated finding accuracy was 89.1% vs 91.8% (2017–2024 vs 2025–2026; difference 2.7 percentage points; 95% CI by article cluster bootstrap, −5.6 to 10.6). Collection runs executed June 27–July 30, 2026; the update window overlaps this period, so the update consists of articles not previously retrieved rather than only articles published after collection ended. No candidate was selected by hand. Accuracy of the update cases was not rated by people; drift of hosted models on fixed inputs was not tested.

## Curriculum linking implementation details

- Assertion rules searched within the same clause, up to seven words before and five words after a matching phrase, for negation, resolution, differential, suspected, and historical cues.
- Median linked cases per eligible topic is 43 (range, 0–891); excluding the unlinked topic changes the minimum to one.
